Monthly management

Social media

Monthly plans with a minimum 3-month commitment. Posts are counted per month and spread across the networks in the plan.

  • Essential

    1 social network · 8 posts · 2 short videos edited from your footage · 8 stories. Planning, scheduling and a monthly summary.

    No comment replies; filming not included

  • Growth

    2 social networks · 12 posts · 4 short videos · 12 stories. Planning, scheduling, replies to comments on working days and a monthly report.

    On-site filming not included

  • Presence+

    2 social networks · 16 posts · 6 short videos · 20 stories. Includes 1 h of on-site filming per month, planning and a report.

    Travel outside the agreed area may be charged

Plan notes

  • Short videos use content you send us, unless filming is explicitly included.
  • Does not include ad spend, replies to private messages, influencers or customer service.
  • Planning starts once we receive the materials and the calendar is approved. Results are not guaranteed.

Online presence

Websites, apps & Google

Projects shaped around your business. We prepare the proposal after a conversation about goals, features and content.

  • Digital strategy & analysis

    A look at your current presence, audience and competitors, and a plan with priorities you can measure.

  • Websites & landing pages

    Clear, fast, mobile-first websites that explain what you do and turn visits into enquiries.

  • Google Business profile

    A complete, up-to-date Google profile so that people nearby find you and know how to reach you.

  • Custom apps

    Digital applications built for your business. We quote after talking about goals, features, platforms, integrations, content and maintenance.

What shapes the quote

Number of screens and features, login or payments, connections to other systems, app store publishing and future maintenance. Hosting, subscriptions, licences and third-party services are listed separately in the proposal.
